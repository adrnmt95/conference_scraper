# Organized Crime Infiltration in Legal Firms and Ownership Networks

## Abstract

This illustrative project studies organized crime infiltration in legal firms using
time-varying business ownership networks. It asks whether temporal graph neural
networks can predict infiltration and how infiltration relates to economic outcomes.
The methodological contribution combines temporal network representation learning,
prediction, and economic interpretation. Relevant audiences include economics of
crime, economic networks, computational social science, and machine learning in the
social sciences. Predictive performance and causal economic effects are distinct
research questions. This example describes a research topic; it makes no claim
about empirical findings.
